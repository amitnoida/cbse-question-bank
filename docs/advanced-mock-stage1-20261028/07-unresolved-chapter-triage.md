## Resolution policy

1. Never assign all questions from a combined chapter to one school chapter.
2. For each question, verify exact concept, correct key/explanation, CBSE 2026-27 curriculum relevance, grade (XI or XII), destination subject, duplicates, and media before any approval.
3. Keep all academically unverified or cross-chapter questions on HOLD/PENDING_REVIEW. Stage 1 does not activate or migrate questions.
4. Cross-class groups like Probability and Electrostatics need question-specific grade assignment; topics without a dedicated school chapter require curriculum/practical/formative assessment review.
5. Full question-level authoritative records remain in `cbse_import.advanced_mock_mapping_20261028` and `cbse_import.advanced_mock_stage1_audit_20261028`.
