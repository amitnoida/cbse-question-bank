## Fast-track instruction adopted
1. **First pass:** Read the stem, options, marked answer, explanation, chapter mapping, CBSE conformity and Advanced-level suitability. Confirm numerical calculations explicitly when straightforward.
2. **Pass only if unambiguous and academically correct on first read**, with relevant Advanced Mock difficulty and sufficiently different question content from already accepted items.
3. **Hold/skip** a question immediately if it is too basic, repeated, ambiguous, image-dependent, incorrectly mapped, incorrectly answered or takes disproportionate effort to fix. No repeated research or rewording of such rows.
4. Continue with the next straightforward question, distributing accepted candidates across chapters.
5. Keep acceptance evidence private until the user's final release decision. Do not confuse earlier **1,257 structural passes** with item-level academic approval.
6. No modifying/deleting live school MCQs, exam-result records or subscriptions.
